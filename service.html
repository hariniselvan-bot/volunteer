Community Development

Neighbourhood clean-ups, skill workshops, and public-space renewals that lift entire streets. We work with resident groups so improvements stick long after the drive ends.

12,500+ families supported

What volunteers do

  • Run weekend clean-up and beautification drives
  • Facilitate skill and literacy workshops
  • Coordinate with resident welfare groups

Environmental Care

Tree plantations, lake restorations, and zero-waste campaigns that give nature room to breathe — with survival tracking so saplings become forests.

48,000+ trees planted

What volunteers do

  • Plant and geo-tag native saplings
  • Join lake and beach restoration crews
  • Lead zero-waste awareness in schools

Program 03

Education & Mentorship

Weekend classes, exam prep, and one-on-one mentoring for first-generation learners. Our mentors stay with students for a full academic year.

6,200+ students mentored

What volunteers do

  • Teach weekend English, maths, or science
  • Mentor a student through the school year
  • Host career-guidance and study-skill sessions

Healthcare & Awareness

Preventive health camps, screenings, and awareness sessions that reach the last mile first — run with licensed medical partners.

900+ health camps held

What volunteers do

  • Assist at screening and vaccination camps
  • Run hygiene and nutrition awareness sessions
  • Support elder-care home visits

Food & Essentials Support

Community kitchens, ration kits, and essentials drives for families facing hard weeks — with dignity-first distribution.

1.1M+ meals served

What volunteers do

  • Cook and serve at community kitchens
  • Pack and deliver monthly ration kits
  • Run clothing and essentials collection drives

  • Coach public-speaking and interview skills
