Stackly is a volunteer organization built on one belief: stronger communities start with people who care.

Our Story

It started with twelve people
and one muddy Sunday.

In 2016, twelve neighbours in Chennai spent a Sunday cleaning a lake bund. They expected a one-off morning. Instead, they found waiting lists of people asking, "when is the next one?"

That question became Stackly. Today, more than ten thousand volunteers across forty cities run programs in community development, environment, education, healthcare, and relief — all coordinated, all measurable, all human.

We are still, at heart, that muddy Sunday: ordinary people, showing up, together.

10,000+ volunteersand every one started with a single day

Our Mission

To connect willing people with meaningful work — making volunteering simple, structured, and deeply human, so that every hour given turns into visible change.

Our Vision

Communities where nobody waits for help to arrive — because neighbours, students, and professionals already stand ready to give their time and skills.

Our Values

What we stand for,
every single day.

01

Communities lead; we listen. Every program starts from a real, stated need.

02

Consistency beats intensity. Small, regular acts outlast grand gestures.
